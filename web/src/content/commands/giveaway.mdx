Requires the moderator role or the Manage Messages permission, and is hidden from members without Manage Server. Giveaways are ended by scheduled tasks or manually with `/giveaway end message-id:<id>`. A giveaway is only ever ended and announced once, even if the timer and a moderator race. If the winner announcement cannot be posted, the bot keeps retrying (about once a minute, up to 60 times) without drawing again. Use reroll to pick new winners from an ended giveaway.

Giveaways belong to the server they were started in: `end` and `reroll` treat a message ID from another server as not found. Winners are drawn only from entrants who are still members of the server, and a reroll never picks someone who already won that giveaway. If nobody is eligible the bot says "No eligible entrants".

Durations are a number plus a unit: `s`, `m` (minutes), `h`, `d`, `w`, case-insensitive and chainable, e.g. `10m`, `2d`, `1h30m`, `2d12h`. A bare number, zero, or a negative is rejected, and nothing is changed when the value is invalid. Giveaways last at most 30 days.
