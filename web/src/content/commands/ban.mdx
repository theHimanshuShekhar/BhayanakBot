Requires moderator access. A duration creates a temporary ban entry for the expiration task to reverse later, retrying if Discord is temporarily unreachable. The latest action wins: a new ban or temporary ban replaces the member's earlier active ban, so an old temporary ban's expiry cannot unban them, and `/unban` closes any active ban too.

Durations are a number plus a unit: `s`, `m` (minutes), `h`, `d`, `w`, case-insensitive and chainable, e.g. `10m`, `2d`, `1h30m`, `2d12h`. A bare number, zero, or a negative is rejected, and nothing is changed when the value is invalid. Temporary bans last at most 1 year.
