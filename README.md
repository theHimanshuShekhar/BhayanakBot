# BhayanakBot

**Your server's worst behavior, automated.**

BhayanakBot is the one bot the Bhayanak server runs on. It keeps order, plays the music, counts everyone's XP, runs the giveaways, and talks back when you mention it, in a voice that's somewhere between a friend and a roast.

No pile of single-purpose bots. No dashboard to log into. Set it up with one command and get on with your day.

👉 **[See every command at bot.bhayanak.net](https://bot.bhayanak.net)**

---

## Why BhayanakBot?

### 🛡️ Moderation that keeps receipts
Ban, kick, mute, warn, and purge, and every action gets a numbered case you can look up later with `/case` and `/history`. Temporary mutes and bans lift themselves on time. Auto-mod catches spam, bad links, and mass mentions, and anti-raid notices when too many accounts join at once.

### 🎵 Music for the voice channel
Search for a song or paste a link, then queue, skip, shuffle, loop, and turn it up. Buttons on the now-playing card do the same. Only people in the bot's voice channel can change what's playing, so nobody skips your song from across the server.

### ⭐ Levels worth chasing
Chatting earns XP. Check where you stand with `/rank`, see who's on top with `/leaderboard`, and let roles hand themselves out when someone reaches a level.

### 🤖 A bot that talks back
Mention it and it answers, using what's been said in the channel and what it knows about the people in it. Every so often, when #general is busy, it joins in on its own. It's playful, never actually mean, and it stays quiet rather than posting something broken.

### 🧠 It knows the regulars
Over time the bot builds a short profile of the server and of each regular from the conversation in #general, and uses it to sound like it's been around. See yours with `/personality view user`, or the whole server's with `/personality view guild`.

### 📝 Catch up in seconds
Missed the last two hours? `/summarize` reads the channel and tells you what happened, naming who said what.

### 🕵️ Guess Who
The bot pulls a real message out of the server's history. Name who wrote it before you run out of guesses.

### 🎉 Giveaways and polls
Start a timed giveaway or a button poll in one command. Winners are drawn from people who are still in the server, and a reroll never picks the same person twice.

### 💬 Autoresponders
Have the bot answer a word, a phrase, or a pattern. Pull parts of the message into the reply, limit it to certain channels, or let the AI write the answer.

### ⛏️ Game servers at a glance
`/minecraft` shows who's on mc.bhayanak.net, the modpack version, the live map, and the mods you need. The Minecraft and Palworld player lists show up as channels in the sidebar, so you can see who's online without asking.

### 🔕 Pings only when they're for you
The bot doesn't ping anyone, except the person a reminder is for, someone who just levelled up, or a giveaway winner.

### 🧰 The little things
Reminders, AFK notes, `/snipe` for the message someone just deleted, welcome and goodbye messages, a starboard, and `/help` for everything else.

---

## Where it works

Most of BhayanakBot works in any server it's added to. The AI replies (AI autoresponders included), `/summarize`, `/personality`, and `/guess_who` are only for the Bhayanak server.

---

## How it works

1. **Add BhayanakBot** to your server from [bot.bhayanak.net](https://bot.bhayanak.net).
2. **Set it up** with `/config`: a log channel, a muted role, welcome messages, whatever you need.
3. **Type `/help`** and start using it.

That's all there is to it.
